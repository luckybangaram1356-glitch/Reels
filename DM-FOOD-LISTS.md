# DM Food Lists — Weight Gain & Weight Loss

Breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack options people can pick from. Each list is
**3 messages**: send them one after another. Instagram cuts off a DM at 1000
characters, and each message here stays under 700.

Suggested keywords (set to exact match in ManyChat): **MASS** for weight gain,
**SLIM** for weight loss. You can also send them as a follow-up to anyone who comments FOOD.

```

1. 2 roti + veg curry + 2-egg bhurji
2. 150g grilled chicken / fish + big salad + ½ cup rice

4. 40g soya chunks curry + 1 roti + veg

• Eat ~300–500 kcal less than your normal
• Protein in every meal (keeps you full, saves muscle)
• Oil: 2–3 tsp a day, measure it with a spoon
• No sugar in tea / coffee, no cool drinks, no packaged snacks
• 8,000 steps a day + 3 L water + 7 hrs sleep
• Aim for −0.5 to −1 kg a week. Faster usually means losing muscle.

Save cheskondi 📌 Inka tips kosam follow avvandi 💪
```

> Calories and protein are approximate. Portions depend on the person's
