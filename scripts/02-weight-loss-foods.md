## Script 2.1 — "5 foods that help you lose belly fat"

**Body**
1. 🗣️ "Eggs — high protein keeps you full for hours."
   🎥 Boiled eggs sliced · 📝 🥚 EGGS — STAY FULL LONGER
2. 🗣️ "Cucumber — almost zero calories, tons of water."
   🎥 Crunchy cucumber slices · 📝 🥒 ~15 KCAL / 100g
3. 🗣️ "Oats — fiber that kills cravings."
   🎥 Oats bowl with fruits · 📝 🥣 OATS — HIGH FIBER

   🎥 Grilled paneer / chicken · 📝 🍗 PROTEIN = KEEP MUSCLE
5. 🗣️ "Green tea or black coffee — zero-calorie drinks instead of sugary ones."
   🎥 Pouring green tea · 📝 🍵 SWAP SUGARY DRINKS

🗣️ "Remember — no food burns fat alone. A calorie deficit does. Follow for more."
📝 DEFICIT = FAT LOSS ✅ FOLLOW

📝 SAME CALORIES 😳

**Body**
1. 🗣️ "This is volume eating — big portions, low calories."
   🎥 Big salad bowl · 📝 VOLUME EATING 🥗

   🎥 Spooning veggies onto plate · 📝 ½ PLATE = VEGGIES

## Script 2.3 — "Stop eating these if you want to lose weight"

**Length:** ~25s

**Hook (0–3s)**
🎥 Red ❌ over a cold drink bottle
🗣️ "You're working out daily but not losing weight? These might be why."
📝 STOP THESE ❌

**Body**
1. 🗣️ "Sugary drinks — one bottle can be 200+ calories."
   🎥 Cold drink → swap to lemon water · 📝 ❌ COLD DRINKS → ✅ LEMON WATER
2. 🗣️ "Fried snacks — swap for roasted chana."
   🎥 Samosa → roasted chana · 📝 ❌ FRIED → ✅ ROASTED CHANA
3. 🗣️ "Packed fruit juice — eat the whole fruit instead."
   🎥 Juice box → whole orange · 📝 ❌ JUICE → ✅ WHOLE FRUIT
4. 🗣️ "Late-night junk — have a glass of milk or curd instead."
   🎥 Midnight chips → bowl of curd · 📝 ❌ NIGHT JUNK → ✅ CURD

**CTA (last 3s)**
🗣️ "Share this with someone who needs it."
📝 SHARE WITH A FRIEND 📤

**Caption:** Small swaps = big results 🔁 Which one are you guilty of?
