## Script format

Every script follows the same structure:

- **Hook (0–3s)** — the line that stops the scroll. Say it fast, show the food/result immediately.
- **Body** — one short line per cut (2–4s each). `🗣️` = voiceover, `🎥` = what to show, `📝` = on-screen text.
  Timestamps assume a normal speaking pace (~2.5 words/sec).
- **CTA (last 4s)** — a save or share line written for that script. Plans, recipes and
  lists say "save this"; mistakes, myths and tips say "share with a friend who…".
- **Caption + hashtags** — paste-ready.

## Shooting tips for this style

- Cut every 2–3 seconds; no shot longer than 4s.
- Put big bold on-screen text for every point — most people watch muted.
- Show the actual food on a plain background (kitchen counter / wooden board).
- Use a trending upbeat audio at low volume under the voiceover.
- Keep numbers on screen (grams of protein, calories) — they get saves.

> Nutrition numbers are approximate (per 100 g unless stated) and for general
> fitness content — not medical advice.
