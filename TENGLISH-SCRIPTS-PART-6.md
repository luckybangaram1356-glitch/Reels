# Reel Scripts — Part 6 (Tenglish) · 6 new formats, 35 scripts

Full scripts for every idea in [REEL-IDEAS.md](REEL-IDEAS.md). Same setup as
the "5 foods" reels: you sit in the gym, talk to camera, and pop-ups show up
beside you. Each reel runs ~25s: hook 0–3s · 5 points 3–18s · CTA 18–23s ·
outro 23–25s. Each one ends with a comment keyword and a ready DM reply.

🗣️ = what you say on camera (Tenglish) · 📝 = on-screen pop-up / subtitle

**Auto-DM tip:** set every keyword to **exact match** in ManyChat. If it is
set to "contains", short keywords fire on the wrong comments (e.g. PRE inside
"PREWORKOUT").

# Format A — Myth or Fact? (rapid fire)

**How to film:** say the belief, pause half a second, then the verdict pops up
big and centred (red ❌ MYTH or green ✅ FACT) as you give the one-line answer.
The left pop-up shows the claim.

> 🏋️ Gym myths, explained
> 1. Sweat is just your body cooling down. Fat loss comes from a calorie deficit.
> 2. Women have far less testosterone, so lifting builds a strong, toned body, not a bulky one.
