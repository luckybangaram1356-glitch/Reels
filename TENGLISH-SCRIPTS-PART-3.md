# Reel Scripts — Part 3 (Tenglish) · "5 foods for X" talking-head format

14 scripts in the format of the reference reel ("5 seeds for hair"): you sit
on camera in the gym, name 5 foods one by one, and point up at a pop-up icon
for each. The ending asks "eppudu teeskovali? ela teeskovali?" and tells
viewers to **comment a keyword**, then auto-DMs them the details.

🗣️ = what you say on camera (Tenglish) · 📝 = on-screen pop-up / subtitle

---

## How the format works (copy this for every reel)

| Time | What happens | On screen |
|------|--------------|-----------|
| 0–3s | **Hook.** Talk straight to camera with the problem and the promise. | Nothing, or one subtitle |
| 3–18s | **5 foods, 3s each.** Say the food, then its one-line benefit, pointing up as you say it. | **Top-left:** food photo in a bowl + label (e.g. PUMPKIN SEEDS). **Top-right:** benefit icon + label (e.g. HAIRFALL), shown when you say the benefit |
| 18–23s | **Comment CTA.** "Ivi eppudu teeskovali? Ela teeskovali? Comment '___'." | White subtitles word by word, then `comment` in handwriting + the **'KEYWORD'** in big red |
| 23–25s | **Outro.** Walk out of frame, then cut to your logo card. | Your name/logo in red on black |

**Filming:**
- One fixed shot, phone at eye level, sitting on a gym bench with machines behind you. Wear a tank top.
- Don't cut away from your face. Only the pop-ups change.
- Pop-ups: cut-out food photo in a bowl with a white uppercase label under it. Make them pop in, not fade.
- Set up an auto-DM tool (like ManyChat) so everyone who comments the keyword gets the **DM reply** below. Because it promises a DM, this CTA gets more comments than "save this".
