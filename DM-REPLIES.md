## EGGS — Eggs gurinchi 5 myths

```
Hi! Comment chesinanduku thanks 🙌 Ide mee plan 👇

🥚 Egg facts
Yolk: for most healthy people, dietary cholesterol from eggs barely moves blood cholesterol. The yolk has vitamin D, B12 and choline.
Brown vs white: just the hen breed. Nutrition is the same.
Raw vs cooked: your body absorbs ~90% of cooked egg protein vs ~50% raw, and raw eggs carry salmonella risk. Always cook them.
Summer: there's no "heat" effect. Eat them all year.
How many: 2–3 whole eggs a day is fine for most healthy people. Add extra whites for more protein.
High cholesterol, diabetes or heart disease? Ask your doctor about yolks.
```
