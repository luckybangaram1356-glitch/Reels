# Reel Scripts — Part 4 (Tenglish) · "5 foods for X" talking-head format

15 more scripts in the same format as Part 3: you talk to camera, name 5 foods
and point up at a pop-up for each, then end with "comment KEYWORD". See the
format and filming guide at the top of
[TENGLISH-SCRIPTS-PART-3.md](TENGLISH-SCRIPTS-PART-3.md).

🗣️ = what you say on camera (Tenglish) · 📝 = on-screen pop-up / subtitle
Every script runs ~25s: hook 0–3s · 5 foods 3–18s · CTA 18–23s · outro 23–25s.
