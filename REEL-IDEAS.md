# New Reel Ideas — 6 formats, 36 ideas

Same setup as the "5 foods" reels (sit in the gym, talk to camera, pop-ups),
so you can film all of these without new gear. Each idea has a ready Tenglish
hook and a comment keyword for the auto-DM. None of these topics repeat
Parts 1–5.

Mix the formats: if every reel is "5 foods", people start scrolling past.
A good weekly mix is 3 "5 foods", 1 myth/fact, 1 versus and 1 comment reply.

---

Say a common belief, pause, and then a big ✅ FACT or ❌ MYTH pops up as you
answer in one line. 5 per reel, about 4s each. Debates in the comments push the reel to more people.
