# Gym Reel Ideas — Telugu (Tenglish) · 7 formats, 44 ideas

These are about **training in the gym**: exercises, form, plans, gym life and
motivation. Most earlier reels were about food, so these give the page a
better balance. Every idea has a Tenglish hook to say in the first 3 seconds
and a comment keyword for the auto-DM. None of them repeat the topics already
covered in Parts 1–7 or [REEL-IDEAS.md](REEL-IDEAS.md). Those already covered
5 gym myths, beginner mistakes, home workout, morning vs evening, 40+,
supplements, walking vs running and back stretches.

All of these can be filmed in the gym with a phone on a tripod. For form
reels, ask a friend to film from the side.

A good weekly mix: 2 gym reels from this file, 2 "5 foods" reels, 1 myth/versus
and 1 comment reply.

## Format G — Form check: ❌ Wrong vs ✅ Right

Split screen or back-to-back clips. First do the wrong rep (light weight, so it
stays safe) with a red ❌, then the right rep with a green ✅. Give one cue per mistake.
People save these and send them to their gym partner.

| # | Hook (Tenglish) | The 3 fixes | Keyword |
|---|-----------------|-------------|---------|
| G1 | "Squat ila chestunnara? Mokallu paadavutayi." | Knees follow toes · Chest up, no rounding · Full foot on floor, heels down | SQUAT |
| G2 | "Bench press lo 90% mandi chese mistake idi." | Shoulder blades squeezed · Bar to lower chest, not neck · Feet planted, no bouncing | BENCH |
| G3 | "Deadlift tho back pain vastunda? Idi chudandi." | Bar close to legs · Flat back, hips not too low · Push the floor, don't yank | DEADLIFT |
| G4 | "Shoulder press chesetappudu ee tappu cheyyakandi." | Don't over-arch lower back · Elbows slightly forward · Full lockout overhead | PRESS |
| G5 | "Bicep curl lo body oopatam aapandi." | Elbows fixed at sides · Slow down, 2–3 sec · Lower weight, full range | CURL |
| G6 | "Lat pulldown neck venaka laagutunnara? Aapandi." | Pull to upper chest · Lean back slightly · Lead with elbows, not hands | LAT |

## What to film first

1. **J1 (6 rakala manushulu)**: everyone tags a friend. Easiest way to reach new people.
2. **G1 (squat form)**: saved and sent to gym partners, and it shows you know your stuff.
3. **I1 (gym first day)**: starts the "Gym 101" series for beginners.
4. **L4 (creatine and kidneys)**: a big question in Telugu comments. Keep the answer careful and general.
5. **M2 (90 days, Day 1)**: start now so people keep coming back for updates.

## Filming tips for gym reels

- Film form reels from the **side** at hip height. Front angles hide most mistakes.
- Use light weight for the ❌ wrong reps, and never film a dangerous rep heavy.
- Ask before filming other members, or film at quiet hours.
- Put sets × reps on screen, because people screenshot them.
- Keep cuts to 2–3 seconds, like the other reels.
